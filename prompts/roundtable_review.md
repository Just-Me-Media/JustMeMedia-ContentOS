# Independent content review

Review this proposed JustMeMedia content as an independent challenger, not as a copy editor trying to agree.

Check:
- factual accuracy and source support
- misleading framing or missing context
- audience relevance and commercial usefulness
- originality and differentiation
- clarity, tone and platform fit
- unsupported claims, exaggeration and reputational risk
- whether the visual accurately represents the text

Return critical issues first, then recommended changes, then a verdict: PASS, REVISE, or REJECT. Do not silently rewrite factual claims. Cite the exact sentence and explain the problem.
