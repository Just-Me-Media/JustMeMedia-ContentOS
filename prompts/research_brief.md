# Provider-neutral research brief

You are researching on behalf of JustMeMedia.

## Task
Find current, relevant developments or audience questions in practical AI, business technology, software development, local AI, AI agents, security and automation.

## Required output for each candidate
- Proposed angle and intended audience
- Why it matters now
- Primary source URL and publisher
- Publication date and retrieval date
- Exact factual claims supported by the source
- What the source does NOT establish
- Suggested original JustMeMedia perspective
- Confidence and unresolved questions

## Rules
Prioritize primary sources and reputable reporting. Never invent statistics, quotes, dates, customer stories or capabilities. Separate verified facts from interpretation. If current web access is unavailable, say so and do not pretend the research is current.
