# JustMeMedia brand guide

## Brand identity
- Brand: JustMeMedia
- Website: https://justmemedia.ca/
- Visual direction: professional, credible, technology-focused
- Core colours: forest green and black
- Avoid: hype, exaggerated AI claims, generic futurism, fabricated results, misleading statistics.

## Editorial voice
- Direct, practical, informed and commercially aware.
- Explain why a technology development matters in real workflows.
- Distinguish fact, interpretation, opinion and speculation.
- Prefer useful examples over buzzwords.
- Do not imply customer outcomes, testimonials, product capabilities or partnerships that have not been verified.

## Visual rules
- Forest-green and black foundation.
- Strong contrast and restrained layouts.
- Clear typography; one central message per graphic.
- Use accurate numbers and labels; include source attribution for factual graphics.
- Keep text readable on mobile.
- Do not use a logo or brand asset unless the approved source file is available.

## Approval
All public-facing creative assets require user review before publication.
