# Content strategy — initial working version

## Objective
Build recognition, trust, relevant audience relationships and commercially useful opportunities for JustMeMedia.

## Primary audience hypothesis
Owners, operators and decision-makers at small and medium-sized businesses who want to understand and apply AI and practical technology without hype.

This is a hypothesis to validate through audience conversations and performance data, not a proven audience profile.

## Primary channel
LinkedIn is the primary business-development channel. Other established channels extend reach with platform-specific adaptations.

## Content pillars
1. Practical AI implementation for small and medium-sized businesses.
2. AI agents, workflow automation, permissions and security.
3. Local AI, open models, privacy and provider choice.
4. Software building, testing, code review and lessons from real development.
5. Relevant JustMeMedia products and genuine build-in-public updates.

## Weekly cadence
Prepare 3–4 original content opportunities each week. Adapt only to platforms where the format and audience fit.

## Editorial quality gate
- Is the topic relevant to the intended audience?
- Is the factual basis supported by reliable sources?
- Are statistics correctly described and dated?
- Is opinion clearly distinguishable from fact?
- Does the post add an explanation, practical takeaway or original perspective?
- Is the content genuinely JustMeMedia-relevant?
- Is the post materially different from recent content?

## Metrics
Track reach, engagement, meaningful comments, profile visits, website clicks, follower growth, inquiries and qualified opportunities. Do not optimize for vanity metrics alone.
